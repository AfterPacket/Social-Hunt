Forensic AI Demasking

This tool uses a multi-stage AI pipeline to create a plausible facial reconstruction. First, visible skin tone, gender cues, and facial hair are sampled from the exposed face area. The gaiter/balaclava region is then pre-filled with the subject's skin colour so the AI sees flesh-coloured pixels rather than a mask — preventing it from generating a replacement covering. Realistic Vision inpainting then fills only the covered region, leaving hair, cap, clothing and background unchanged. It uses visible head shape, ears, temples, cheek contours and jaw position to constrain the reconstructed nose, mouth and lower-face anatomy. The same input produces the same reconstruction seed, so repeated runs do not silently swap in a different face.

Warning: This feature is experimental. No AI can know what a person's face truly looks like beneath a covering — results are plausible reconstructions, not ground truth. Always verify against primary source material.

Forensic Disclaimer
Reconstructions are plausible estimates generated by neural networks — they cannot reveal a person's actual hidden identity and are not ground truth. The SD inpainting step constrains changes to the detected face-covering region only, significantly reducing identity distortion compared to full-image generation.
