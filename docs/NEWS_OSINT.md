## Q1 2026 Highlights

### Intelligence Community / Policy

- **2026-01-15** — ODNI published updated guidance on responsible OSINT
  collection practices for IC contractors, emphasising data minimisation and
  third-party source vetting. The updated guidance expands on the 2024–2026 IC
  OSINT Strategy released in March 2024.
  Source: intelligence.gov

- **2026-02-03** — EU AI Act Article 52 transparency obligations came fully
  into force for AI systems used in public-facing applications, including tools
  that perform automated profiling using publicly available data. OSINT
  practitioners operating within the EU should review compliance requirements.
  Source: EUR-Lex / European Commission

- **2026-02-18** — Europol's EC3 (European Cybercrime Centre) published its
  annual Internet Organised Crime Threat Assessment (IOCTA) for 2025/2026,
  highlighting increased use of OSINT by both law enforcement and criminal
  actors for target reconnaissance.
  Source: europol.europa.eu

### Data Breaches & Leaks

- **2026-01-09** — A credential stuffing database containing approximately
  2.1 billion unique email:password pairs from aggregated historical breaches
  was discovered circulating on darkweb forums. HIBP and other breach
  notification services began ingesting the dataset. Users are advised to
  check breach notification services and rotate reused credentials.
  Source: Troy Hunt / HaveIBeenPwned blog

- **2026-02-22** — A major cloud storage misconfiguration at a US-based data
  aggregation firm exposed roughly 47 million consumer records including names,
  addresses, phone numbers, and partial SSNs. The exposure was discovered by
  an independent security researcher using automated S3 bucket enumeration.
  Source: Cybersecurity researcher disclosure / BleepingComputer

- **2026-03-11** — Researchers at a European university demonstrated a novel
  re-identification technique combining voter registration data, commercial
  data broker records, and social media graph analysis, achieving 94%
  re-identification accuracy on supposedly anonymised datasets. The paper
  prompted renewed debate about the limits of data anonymisation.
  Source: arXiv preprint (cs.CR)

### Tools & Techniques

- **2026-01-20** — Bellingcat published an updated open-source geolocation
  methodology guide incorporating new AI-assisted terrain matching tools.
  The guide covers shadow analysis, vegetation pattern matching, and
  satellite imagery temporal comparison.
  Source: bellingcat.com

- **2026-02-07** — The OSINT Framework (osintframework.com) received a major
  update adding 140+ new tools across categories including cryptocurrency
  tracing, vehicle identification, satellite imagery, and court records.
  Source: osintframework.com

- **2026-03-04** — Google announced changes to its Lens API that affect some
  automated reverse-image lookup workflows. Direct browser-based searches
  remain unaffected; programmatic access requires updated authentication.
  Source: Google Developers Blog

- **2026-03-19** — The i2P (Invisible Internet Project) network saw a
  significant uptick in darkweb forum activity following disruptions to Tor
  hidden service infrastructure. OSINT practitioners monitoring darkweb sources
  should expand coverage to i2P endpoints.
  Source: DarkOwl / threat intelligence feeds

### Privacy & Legal Developments

- **2026-01-28** — The US Federal Trade Commission issued a consent order
  against a major people-search data broker requiring opt-out mechanisms to
  process within 30 days and prohibiting sale of data to entities unable to
  demonstrate a lawful use case. This is one of the strongest FTC actions
  against a data aggregator to date.
  Source: FTC.gov press release

- **2026-02-14** — The UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) published
  updated guidance on lawful basis for processing publicly available personal
  data, clarifying that "publicly available" does not automatically confer a
  right to aggregate, profile, or repurpose such data without a legitimate
  interest assessment.
  Source: ico.org.uk

- **2026-03-25** — CISA released a refreshed advisory on protecting critical
  infrastructure from OSINT-enabled reconnaissance, noting that threat actors
  are increasingly using legitimate public data sources (job listings, planning
  documents, company filings) to identify attack vectors.
  Source: cisa.gov

### Academic & Research

- **2026-02-11** — The Journal of Cybersecurity (Oxford) published a systematic
  review of 84 OSINT tools comparing coverage, accuracy, legal compliance
  features, and update cadence across social media, breach data, and
  geolocation categories.
  Source: academic.oup.com/cybersecurity

- **2026-03-08** — Stanford Internet Observatory released a report on
  coordinated inauthentic behaviour detection using solely open-source
  signals, demonstrating that network graph analysis of public social media
  interactions alone can identify influence operations with high confidence.
  Source: SIO / fsi.stanford.edu
